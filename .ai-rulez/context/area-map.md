## Area Map

Public repository, no product code. `README.md` is the commercial overview; `docs-site/` is the Starlight site served at `pro.xberg.io` on the shared `@xberg-io/docs-theme`.

- The product source and its private engineering notes live in the private `xberg-enterprise` repository. Port customer-facing content only; never link the private repository, internal source paths, issue numbers, staging hosts or customer names. `mise run docs:lint:public` (`scripts/check-public-content.sh`) enforces the denylist in CI.
- Pro vs Enterprise claims follow the shipped capability manifest in the product, not the sales brief; features not yet shipped belong on `overview/roadmap`.
- Sidebar groups other than Overview, Get Started and More are autogenerated per directory; order pages with `sidebar.order` frontmatter.
- The OpenAPI specifications are not vendored here: the API reference loads the public copies published by `xberg-io/sdks`.
- Gates: `mise run docs:check` (build, public-content, lychee links, textlint prose).
